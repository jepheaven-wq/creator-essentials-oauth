Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Inspire Lifetime Creator Essentials ("the tool") is a private, single-user automation that runs on the computer of its operator, who is also the sole owner of the Google Account it connects to. It is not offered to the public as a service and it has no users other than that one person.

1. What data the tool accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly) — to summarise messages that arrive in the owner's own support inbox so the owner can respond to customers.
  • Gmail, send (gmail.send) — to send email from the owner's own address, only when the owner explicitly asks for it (for example, a self-addressed copy of a local review document). It cannot read, delete, or modify messages with this permission.
  • OpenID identity (openid, userinfo.email) — to confirm that the connected account is the owner's own address before any credential is used or replaced.
  • YouTube and YouTube Analytics, read-only — to read view and engagement figures for the owner's own videos and channel.

2. How the data is used

Only to produce local reports for the owner: a support summary, a weekly performance scorecard, and connection-health checks. Message content is treated as data, not as instructions, and no automated reply, refund, purchase, publication, or account change is ever made from it.

3. Storage and security

  • Reports and intermediate files are stored on the owner's own computer.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally in a file restricted to the owner's Windows account, encrypted with keys protected by Windows DPAPI (current user).
  • The tool does not operate any server, database, or cloud storage of its own, and does not transmit data to the operator of this website.

4. Sharing

Google user data is never sold, rented, shared, or transferred to third parties, and is never used for advertising, profiling, or to determine creditworthiness. No human reads the data other than the account owner, because the account owner is the only operator.

5. Retention and deletion

Local reports are kept until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored credential; the local credential file can then be deleted.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Inspire Lifetime Creator Essentials' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

The tool is not directed at children and has no end users other than its owner.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Questions: jepheaven@gmail.com.
