Internal single-user automation

Inspire Lifetime Creator Essentials

Inspire Lifetime Creator Essentials is a private automation tool that runs on its operator's own computer for the Inspire Lifetime YouTube channel and its small creator storefront. It is used by one person: the owner of the account it connects to.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own support inbox to prepare a daily summary of customer messages.
  • Reads public and owner-only YouTube Analytics figures for the owner's own videos.
  • Writes local reports on the owner's computer and keeps a local encrypted copy of its own credentials.
  • Can send the owner a single self-addressed email with a status or review attachment, on request.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or creditworthiness.
  • It does not read anyone else's data, and it does not act on Google accounts other than its owner's.

Google user data

The tool requests the minimum scopes it needs: read-only access to the owner's Gmail for support summaries, read-only access to the owner's YouTube Analytics, and permission to send email from the owner's own address when the owner asks it to. Its use of that data is limited to the practices described in the privacy policy.
